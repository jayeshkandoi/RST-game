RST

Say a word that fits the last one. Never start with R, S or T.

How to play

  1. First player says any word.
  2. Next player says a word that is similar to or makes sense with it.
  3. A word starting with R, S or T puts you out.
  4. Repeated words, unrelated words or running out of time also put you out.
  5. Last player left wins.
